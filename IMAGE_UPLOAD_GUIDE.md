### Tips for Best Visuals:
1. **Format:** Use `.webp` for fast loading speeds and modern web compatibility.
2. **Aspect Ratio:** Keep images square or 4:3 rectangular for a neat, uniform grid display.
3. **Brightness:** Use bright, well-lit, commercial product photography styles with transparent or elegant festive backdrops.
